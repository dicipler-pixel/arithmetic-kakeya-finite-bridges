# Provenance

The five `Kakeya*` modules listed first in the README were published here first; their record is
the `kakeya-public` workflow and `research/kakeya_public/PUBLIC_SOURCE_RECEIPT.json`.

The nine wider-library modules are byte-identical copies from the research repository: the eight
`OperatorFirst/` files from pull request 4 (head `41fbf3b9`) and `MixedIntake/AK_original.lean`
from pull request 3, as recorded in the verified Lean proof atlas of 10 September 2026 (the
Offset handoff archive carries identical copies). They were checked there against Lean v4.33.0.
This repository's `build.yml` is their first public record.
