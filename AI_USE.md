# AI use

The research questions and the mathematics behind this library are Jeromie
Beasley's. AI tools (Claude and ChatGPT) were used to search the literature,
write and repair Lean proofs, and organise this repository.

No statement here rests on an AI's say-so. Every proof is checked by the Lean
kernel on every push, and the false statements in `FalseControls` must be
rejected for the check to pass. What the statements mean, and what they do not
claim, is set out in plain words in `README.md` and `LIMITATIONS.md`.
